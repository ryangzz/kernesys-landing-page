Frequently asked questions

What is an MES?

An MES (Manufacturing Execution System) is the system that records what happens on the production floor: orders, progress, consumption, incidents and traceability.

Does the system adapt to my production process?

Yes. We design it around your actual flow, your stations and the controls you need, instead of asking you to change your process.

Can it connect to my ERP?

Yes, usually through an API, the database or exchange files. We evaluate connections with plant equipment on a case-by-case basis.

How do plant workers enter information?

With tablets, workstation computers, phones or barcode readers, depending on what works best in your plant.
