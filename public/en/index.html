Frequently asked questions

What software development services does Kernesys offer?

Kernesys offers custom software development, WMS solutions for warehouses, TMS for transportation and MES for production, ERP and CRM systems, process automation, systems integration and cloud services (AWS), mobile apps built with Flutter, and system migration and modernization.

Where is Kernesys located?

Kernesys is headquartered in Monterrey, Nuevo León, Mexico, and serves clients from all over the world.

What technologies does Kernesys use?

We choose the technology based on what each project needs. For web systems we use Vue.js, React and Angular, with Laravel, NestJS, FastAPI and Spring Boot; for desktop applications, C# / .NET, C++, Java and Python; for iPhone and Android mobile apps, Flutter; and for analytics and machine learning, Python, PostgreSQL, MongoDB and Elasticsearch. We use AWS for cloud infrastructure.

Does Kernesys develop mobile apps?

Yes, Kernesys develops mobile apps with Flutter, which allows us to build fast, smooth experiences on both iOS and Android from a single, efficient codebase.

How long has Kernesys been in business?

Kernesys is a 100% Mexican company operating since 2019, dedicated to driving the digital transformation of businesses through innovative, tailored technology solutions.

How long does a software project with Kernesys take?

It depends on the complexity. A website can be ready in 2–4 weeks, a mobile app in 6–12 weeks and a complete ERP or CRM system in 3–6 months. We always provide a detailed timeline before starting so you have full visibility into the process.

Does Kernesys offer support and maintenance after launch?

Yes, all our projects include a post-launch support period. We also offer extended maintenance plans with updates, fixes and technical assistance to keep your system running optimally over the long term.

Does Kernesys work with companies outside Monterrey and Mexico?

Yes, we work with clients throughout Mexico, Latin America and the United States. Thanks to our remote work methodologies and collaboration tools, distance is not an obstacle to delivering high-quality software projects.
