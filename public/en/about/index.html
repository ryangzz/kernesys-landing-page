We are a 100% Mexican company that helps other companies organize, automate and digitize their operations with systems built around their real processes.

Kernesys at a glance

  • 100% Mexican company
  • Founded in 2019 in Monterrey, Nuevo León
  • Projects in Mexico, Latin America and the United States

Our mission

To support companies as they grow with technology solutions that transform and improve their processes.

We automate and systematize operations so your team spends less time entering, searching for and reconciling information, and more time growing the business.

“We don't start with technology: first we understand how your company works, and then we choose the tool that best solves it.”

What guides us

The principles we apply to every project.

Process first

Before writing code, we understand how your company operates, what holds it back and what already works well.

Plain talk

We explain scope, costs and timelines in business terms, without unnecessary jargon.

Visible progress

We work in phases and show progress often so we can adjust in time.

Long-term relationships

We stay with you after launch, with support and improvements as your operation grows.

Your data, protected

We take care of access, permissions and backups from the system's design stage.

Systems that grow

We build with what your company will need tomorrow in mind, not just today.
